**3. Formation Radar (0:21).** Formation Radar looks for people and teams before any announcement. There are six candidates today, and all of them are early. The top one is Sameh Khamis, at 44. His GitHub bio now reads "Founder and CTO at Stealth Startup", and it lists NVIDIA, Google and Microsoft Research before that. That's self-reported, and it's labelled that way.

**4. Signal collision (0:40).** Here the radar holds back. It's one signal from one source, GitHub, and no team is linked yet. Convergence is zero. So it stays an early signal; it isn't called a company.

**5. Why now (0:51).** The timeline gives the dated reason it surfaced: on October 20th his profile started saying founder and stealth. Every item links to its source and says whether it's a fact or an inference.

**6. The person (1:03).** The second thread is Kevin Zakka: 272 verified commits to Google DeepMind's MuJoCo repositories. In September he started mjbatch, for running thousands of physics simulations in parallel, and it reached 600 stars in 40 days. That history comes from commit records, not from his bio.

His meeting brief gives thirty-second context, what he's built, five questions and what to verify. Every line is cited and marked fact or inference. It's a template, because no language model is switched on.

**8. Attention gap (1:47).** ShengShu has the biggest attention gap. Its new robotics model, Motus2, gained 616 GitHub stars in 30 days, and the last week ran almost eight times faster than the four before it. Technical momentum is 97; public attention is 26. There are no Hacker News threads, and X and press weren't measured, so they get no credit.

**9. Anti Brief (2:04).** Investigate turns that evidence into an Anti Brief: what they do, why now and what changed. Where the founders are unknown, it says so instead of guessing. Every claim has a source number, and it warns that single-source activity is weaker evidence.

**10. Close (2:21).** Find the technical change early, show the evidence, and say what we don't know. Conviction before consensus.
